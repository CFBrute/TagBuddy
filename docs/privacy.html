Tag Buddy – Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to the Tag Buddy app for Android

In short

  • Everything you save stays on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking. We never receive any of your data.
  • The only network use is link previews: for a web link you scan, read or save, the app asks that website for the page's title, picture and icon. You can switch this off in the app.
  • What previews bring back (titles, pictures, icons) is stored only on your phone. The links you scan and the pages you open are never sent to us, and nothing about your use of the app reaches us.

Who we are

Tag Buddy is a hobby app made by an independent developer. It creates and scans barcodes and QR codes and reads and writes NFC tags. Questions about this policy go to the address under Contact.

What stays on your phone

Codes, text notes and NFC tag dumps you save are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Link previews and site icons are kept in the app's cache so they do not need fetching again. Nothing of this is sent to us; we have no server that could receive it.

You can delete saved items in the app, clear stored previews and icons on the About tab, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved items in your device backup, stored in your own Google account under Google's terms. The app's cache (previews and icons) is not part of that backup. We cannot see or access your backups.

Camera and pictures

The camera is used only while the Scan tab is open, to find codes in the live picture. Frames are analysed on the phone and never stored or sent anywhere. "From image" reads only the picture you pick, on the phone. "Save image" puts a code you made into your gallery (Pictures/Tag Buddy) when you ask for it.

NFC

NFC is used only while the NFC tab is open, for the tag or card you hold to the phone. For payment cards and passports the app reads only the public information that tells what kind of card it is (for example the card brand); it never reads card numbers, names or other personal data, and it never sends anything from a tag or card anywhere. Tag contents you read are shown on screen and saved only if you choose to.

Link previews: the only network use

When a web link appears in the app (a scanned code, a tag you read, or a saved item), the app can show a preview card with the page's title, picture and site icon. To get them it contacts:

  • The website of the link: the page itself, and the site's icon files (its homepage and, for an address like shop.example.com, the parent site example.com).
  • The site's own preview service for a few large sites (YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, X, Reddit, Spotify, SoundCloud, Flickr, Dailymotion, Bluesky). The link is sent to that same company's public "oEmbed" address.
  • The places the page points to: when a page has a preview picture, it is loaded from wherever the page says. When a page has no preview data, the app may render the page out of sight to take a picture of it; the page then loads as in a browser, including any services the website itself uses.

All of this stays on your phone. The links themselves, the pages' titles, descriptions, pictures and icons, and the pictures the app takes of pages are kept only in the app's storage on your phone. None of it is ever sent to us: the app has no server of ours to send it to, and it reports nothing about which links you scan, preview or open.

Like any web request, these requests reveal your phone's IP address and the link being previewed to the sites involved (never to us), under their own privacy policies. Websites may set cookies as they would in a browser; only those websites can read them, and clearing the app's storage removes them. Requests identify themselves honestly as Tag Buddy; the app does not pose as a browser or any other service, adds no identifiers of its own, and uses no third-party icon or tracking service.

When a page is rendered for a picture, pop-ups such as cookie banners are hidden in the app's private copy so the picture shows the page. Nothing on the page is clicked, accepted or answered on your behalf. Pages that are a bot check are recognised and not kept; the app does not try to get past them.

Pages you open in the app. When you open a link in the app's own page viewer, the app keeps what that page showed (its title, description, picture and site icon, or a picture of the top of the page) on your phone as that link's preview, unless a better one is stored. Only the page the link led to is used, never pages you go on to. For links that carry a sign-in or one-time code, only the site's icon is kept, because such pages can show personal information. This stays on your phone like every other preview.

Links that carry a sign-in or one-time code (tokens, password resets, unsubscribe or confirm links) are never requested for a preview, because opening them could use the code up. Only the site's icon is fetched for those.

You can switch this off. With "Load link previews automatically" turned off on the About tab, the app makes no network request until you tap a preview card or open the page yourself. Previews already on the phone still show.

Opening a link, viewing a page in the app, sharing an image or file, and sending an email are things you start yourself; they go to the app or website you choose.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting.
  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No selling or sharing of data: we do not receive any to begin with.
  • No location or contacts access, and no reading of your files beyond a picture or dump file you pick.
  • The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste, and written only when you tap Copy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: scanning codes on the Scan tab.
  • NFC: reading and writing tags on the NFC tab.
  • Internet: link previews, as described above.
  • Vibration: a short buzz when a code or tag is read.

Children

Tag Buddy is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
